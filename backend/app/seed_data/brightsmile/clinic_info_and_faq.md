## Is teeth whitening safe?
Professional whitening done by a dentist is safe for most adults. Some people feel temporary sensitivity for 24 to 48 hours. It is not recommended during pregnancy or for children under 16.

## Do you treat children?
Yes, from age 3. Dr. Sneha Iyer sees most of our young patients.

## Emergencies
For severe pain, swelling, bleeding that does not stop, or a knocked-out tooth, call the clinic immediately on +91 80 4111 2233 during opening hours. Outside opening hours, please visit the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Cancellation policy
Please give at least 12 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. You can reschedule by replying to your confirmation message.

## Sterilisation and safety
All instruments are sterilised in Class B autoclaves and every patient gets a fresh disposable kit.
