# BrightSmile Dental Clinic: Treatments and Prices (2026)

All prices are in INR and include GST. Final cost is confirmed by the dentist after the examination.

## Consultation and check-ups
- New patient consultation with full examination and digital X-ray: ₹800.
- Follow-up consultation: ₹400.
- Family check-up package (up to 4 members): ₹2,400.

## Cleaning and gum care
- Scaling and polishing (cleaning): ₹1,500. Recommended every 6 months.
- Deep cleaning for gum disease (per quadrant): ₹3,000.

## Cosmetic dentistry
- Professional in-clinic teeth whitening (one 60-minute session): ₹12,000. Results last 12 to 24 months.
- Take-home whitening kit with custom trays: ₹7,500.
- Composite veneers: ₹6,000 per tooth. Porcelain veneers: ₹18,000 per tooth.

## Orthodontics
- Metal braces (full treatment, 12 to 18 months): ₹45,000.
- Ceramic braces: ₹65,000.
- Clear aligners: from ₹1,20,000 depending on the case. Free aligner assessment with a 3D scan.

## Restorative treatment
- Tooth-coloured filling: ₹1,800 per tooth.
- Root canal treatment (single sitting where possible): ₹6,500 for front teeth, ₹8,500 for molars.
- Zirconia crown: ₹14,000.
- Dental implant with crown: from ₹38,000 per tooth.

## Children's dentistry
- Child check-up and fluoride application: ₹1,000.
- Pit and fissure sealants: ₹900 per tooth.
