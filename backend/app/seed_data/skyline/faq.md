# Skyline Realty: Frequently Asked Questions

## Are your projects RERA approved?
Yes. Every Skyline project is registered with Karnataka RERA. Registration numbers are on each project page and in the brochure.

## Can NRIs buy?
Yes. NRIs can buy residential property in India. We help with power of attorney, NRE/NRO payment routing and video site visits.

## Is there a cancellation policy?
If a booking is cancelled within 15 days, the booking amount is refunded minus ₹25,000 processing charges. After the agreement is signed, cancellation terms follow the RERA agreement for sale.

## What are the maintenance charges?
Maintenance is ₹4.5 per sq ft per month for Orchid Heights, ₹6 per sq ft per month for Lakeview, and ₹5 per sq ft per month for Greens Villas.

## Are pets allowed?
Yes, all Skyline communities are pet friendly and Orchid Heights has a dedicated pet park.

## How far is the metro from Orchid Heights?
Kadugodi Tree Park metro station on the Purple Line is 1.2 km away, about a 4-minute drive.

## Do you offer rentals?
Skyline Realty sells homes only. For rentals in our completed projects, the resident association maintains a listing; our team can share the contact.

## What is the carpet area efficiency?
Carpet area is about 74% of the super built-up area in our apartments.

## Can I customise the interiors?
Buyers in Towers C and D of Orchid Heights and in Lakeview can choose flooring and kitchen options until the structure is complete.
