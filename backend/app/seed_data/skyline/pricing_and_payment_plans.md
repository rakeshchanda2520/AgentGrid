# Skyline Realty: Pricing, Offers and Payment Plans

## Booking amount
- A booking amount of ₹2 lakh confirms a unit. It is adjusted against the first instalment.
- Booking can be done online after a site visit, or at the sales office.

## Payment plans
- Construction-linked plan (CLP): 10% on booking, 10% on agreement, the rest in 8 stage-wise instalments as construction progresses. Available for Towers C and D of Orchid Heights and for Lakeview.
- Down payment plan: pay 95% within 45 days of booking and get a 4% discount on the base price.
- Subvention plan (Lakeview only, until 31 December 2026): pay 20% now and 80% on possession; Skyline pays the pre-EMI interest until possession.

## Current offers (valid until 31 December 2026)
- Orchid Heights ready towers: free modular kitchen worth ₹4.5 lakh on bookings made within 7 days of a site visit.
- Greens Villas: stamp duty waived for the first 10 bookings of Phase 2.
- No festive offer can be combined with the down payment discount.

## Home loans
- Pre-approved by SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and LIC Housing Finance.
- Home loan rates in October 2026 start from 8.35% p.a. (bank's discretion).
- Our loan desk helps with documentation at no charge.

## Additional costs
- Registration and stamp duty: about 6.6% of the agreement value (Karnataka rates).
- GST: 5% for under-construction homes; no GST on ready-to-move-in homes with an Occupancy Certificate.
- Maintenance deposit: ₹75 per sq ft for 24 months, collected at possession.
- Car parking is included in the price; a second parking slot costs ₹3.5 lakh.

## Site visits
- Site visits run Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM (Saturday until 5:00 PM). Sundays by special appointment only.
- Free pick-up and drop within 15 km of the project for scheduled visits.
- A visit takes about an hour, including a show-flat walkthrough.
